# Austin prospect list

Watch stores only: the shops in the Austin area that sell or service
watches, Rolex stores first. 29 in all. The same list, with tracker
columns, is in `austin-prospects.csv`: import it into Google Sheets and
keep one row per shop.

Built from web listings on October 21, 2026. Most details are the shops'
own marketing, press coverage or review-site entries, so confirm the
address, hours and whether the shop sells Rolex before each visit.

**Contacts.** Owners or managers are named for 15 shops, from press
coverage and the shops' own pages; several sources are years old, so
confirm the name when you call or visit. Only Bernard Watch publishes an
email address. For the rest, ask for the owner's email on the visit or the
first call; do not guess addresses, since a wrong one bounces and a guessed
one reads as spam.

## Confirm before the first touch

- **Not found in search:** Don Wilson Jewelers, Moises Jewelry and Rees
  Watch Repair came from review sites only. Confirm each exists before a
  visit or mailer.
- **Location to confirm:** Executive Timepieces lists no street address.
- **Same company?** DJP's founders are named for a Houston business of that
  name; confirm the Austin store belongs to it.
- **Head office decisions:** Marc Robinson Jewelers is one of about 20
  Texas stores, and the Omega boutique buys through Omega. Expect the local
  answer to be "ask head office."
