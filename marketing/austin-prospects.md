# Austin prospect list

Watch stores only: the shops in the Austin area that sell or service
watches, Rolex stores first. Thirty in all. The same list, with tracker
columns, is in `austin-prospects.csv`: import it into Google Sheets and
keep one row per shop.

Built from web listings on October 21, 2026. Most details are the shops' own
marketing or review-site entries, so confirm the address, hours and whether
the shop sells Rolex before each visit. Rows marked "confirm" lacked a
reliable address. Owners' names, emails and Instagram handles are for week 1.

**Totals:** 2 Rolex stores, 21 tier A (pre-owned Rolex dealers and Rolex
service), 6 tier B (jewelers with a watch business), 1 tier C (a brand
boutique).

**First touches:** visits in weeks 2 to 4 for central Austin; mail in week 2
for Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown and Lakeway; email in week 2 for the
rest of tier A and week 6 for tiers B and C.

## If thirty is not enough

The plan's funnel assumed 50 prospects. Thirty watch stores means each one
counts for more: four signed is about one in eight, so every shop gets a
visit or a call, not just an email. If January starts with fewer than two
signed, add the watch stores of San Antonio by mail and phone.
