# San Antonio, Houston and Dallas-Fort Worth prospect list

Watch stores only, Rolex stores first, in the three Texas cities beyond
Austin. 32 in all. The same list, with tracker columns and a City
column, is in `texas-prospects.csv`: import it into the same Google Sheet
as the Austin list, as a second tab.

Built from web listings on October 23, 2026. Most details come from the
shops' own sites, press coverage and Rolex's dealer pages, so confirm
addresses and hours before calling or visiting.

**Contacts.** Owners or leaders are named for 22 of 32 shops.
Several sources are a few years old, so confirm the name on the first call.
No shop on this list publishes an owner's email; ask for it on the call.

**These cities are reached by phone, email and mail first**, with one day
trip each once a shop says yes to a meeting (see `retail-outreach.md`).

**Groups to pitch once, not store by store:**

- **Bachendorf's:** four Rolex stores in Dallas-Fort Worth, all under
  Lawrence Bock.
- **Lee Michaels:** two San Antonio stores, run by the Berg family from
  Baton Rouge.
- **Deutsch:** two Houston stores, both under Lance Deutsch.
- **Zadok:** Houston and Austin, owned by the Zadok family.
- **deBoulle:** Houston, with a Dallas store as well, under the Boulles.
