# San Antonio, Houston and Dallas-Fort Worth prospect list

Watch stores only, Rolex stores first, in the three Texas cities beyond
Austin. 33 in all. The same list, with tracker columns and a City
column, is in `texas-prospects.csv`: import it into the same Google Sheet
as the Austin list, as a second tab.

Built from web listings on October 23, 2026. Most details come from the
shops' own sites and Rolex's dealer pages, so confirm addresses and hours
before calling or visiting. Owners' names and emails are still to find.

**These cities are reached by phone, email and mail first**, with one day
trip each once a shop says yes to a meeting (see `retail-outreach.md`).

**Groups to pitch once, not store by store:** Bachendorf's (four Rolex
stores in Dallas-Fort Worth), Lee Michaels (two in San Antonio, more across
the South), Deutsch (two in Houston), Marc Samuels (three in Dallas-Fort
Worth), and Zadok (Houston and Austin).
