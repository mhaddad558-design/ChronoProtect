# Supplier research

First pass, November 8, 2026. Visits are on hold until a supplier and the
product are confirmed (`visits-on-hold.md`). This is the shortlist to
request quotes and samples from.

**How far to trust it.** MOQs and prices come from the suppliers' own
pages and marketplace listings, found through search. None is confirmed.
Contacts marked *listed* came from a trade-show brochure, the supplier's
own site or a directory. Everything else goes through the site's contact
form or the marketplace storefront. This session could not open supplier
websites directly, so treat every number as a starting point for the
quote. A session on the new "ChronoProtect" cloud environment can open
those sites and fill the gaps.

## What we are buying

One kit is five things. They can come from one factory or from three:

| Part | What it is | Who makes it |
|---|---|---|
| Film | Clear TPU protection film, self-healing, removable adhesive. Gloss and a matte "Stealth" finish | PPF film makers |
| Cutting | Each reference's pieces kiss-cut on one numbered sheet, from our templates | A converter, or us with a plotter |
| Box | Rigid box, deep forest green, bronze crest in foil, with an insert | Rigid box makers |
| Swabs | One wet (alcohol) and one dry wipe per kit, sealed sachets | Wipe makers |
| Card | The fitting and care card, printed both sides | The box maker, as an insert |

**The decision that shapes the rest: who cuts.** We sell 69 references.
A converter quotes an MOQ *per design*: JJT asks 3,000 pieces per model, so
69 references would need about 207,000 pieces before the first sale. The
other route is to buy film by the roll and cut each kit to order on our own
plotter, which is what the site already promises ("cut individually to
your exact reference"). Then the only MOQs are on rolls, boxes, wipes and
cards. Recommendation: **cut in-house**, buy the rest. Ask the converters
anyway: some will cut short runs from our files, which helps the five or
six best-selling references.

## Film

Watch film is thinner than car film. FineWatchCare quotes 0.16 mm for its
watch film; car PPF is usually 7.5 to 8 mil (about 0.19 to 0.2 mm). Ask
each maker for a 6 to 7.5 mil grade, and for gloss and matte.

### Resin makers (to qualify a film maker, not to buy from)

Most good film is coated on **Covestro Desmopan UP** (Taiwan line) or
**Lubrizol ESTANE** TPU. Ask every film maker which resin it uses; Lubrizol
runs a program for film makers that use 100% ESTANE.

## Next steps

1. Send the request below to the film makers (ELOV, Shichuang, Xushield,
   Hexis, STEK) and the one-stop makers (JJT, Kaevlen, ULELA, Tuoli).
2. Fit the samples on a steel watch: clarity, the matte finish, edge lift
   on the lugs and clasp after a week, and removal with no residue.
3. Decide who cuts. In-house needs a plotter (Graphtec or Summa class)
   and the 69 templates.
4. Then the box (Zhibang, CBP) and swabs (from the converter, or
   SYWIPE / Hangzhou).

## Request for quote and samples

> Subject: Quote and samples: TPU protection film for watches
>
> Hello,
>
> I run ChronoProtect+, a studio in Austin, Texas that makes protective
> film kits for luxury watches: clear TPU film, cut to each watch
> reference, applied by appointment. We are choosing a supplier for our
> first production and would like a quote and samples.
>
> What we need:
> - Clear TPU protection film, self-healing, with a removable adhesive
>   that leaves no residue. Gloss and matte (satin) finishes. A thinner
>   grade than car PPF if you make one (6 to 7.5 mil).
> - [For converters] Kiss-cut sheets from our DXF templates, about
>   15 to 30 small pieces per sheet, numbered. Short runs per design.
> - [If you offer it] A rigid retail box with a foil logo, a printed
>   insert card, and one wet alcohol pad and one dry wipe per kit.

> 1. Your MOQ for each item, and per design for cut sheets.
> 2. Price at your MOQ, at 1,000 kits and at 5,000 kits.
> 3. Which TPU resin the film uses, its thickness, and its warranty
>    against yellowing.
> 4. Sample cost and lead time, and production lead time.
> 5. Shipping terms to Austin, Texas (EXW, FOB or DDP).
>
> Thank you,
>
