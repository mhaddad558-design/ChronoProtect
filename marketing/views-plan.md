# Views plan: 2 million a month

The goal is 2,000,000 Instagram views a month, with a high volume of Reels
to sample the audience: many hooks, formats and worlds, so the ones that
travel show themselves quickly. Higgsfield gives 1,000 credits a month, so
most of the volume has to come from material that costs no credits.

## The numbers

| Period | Views needed | Reels at 6 a day | Average views per Reel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day | 66,700 | 6 | 11,100 |
| Week | 466,700 | 42 | 11,100 |
| Month (30 days) | 2,000,000 | 180 | 11,100 |

The average hides how views actually arrive. On a young account most Reels
land in the low thousands, and a handful of breakouts carry the month. A
realistic month at target looks more like this:

| Share of Reels | Views each | Reels | Views |
|---|---|---|---|
| Breakouts (top 3%) | 150,000 to 400,000 | 5 | 1,100,000 |
| Strong (next 12%) | 20,000 to 40,000 | 22 | 600,000 |
| The rest (85%) | about 2,000 | 153 | 300,000 |
| **Total** | | **180** | **2,000,000** |

So the job of six a day is to find the five breakouts a month, then put
paid spend and re-cuts behind them (see `paid-instagram.md`).

## Six-month ramp

2,000,000 a month stays the long-term goal. The owner set a nearer one on
November 1: 500,000 a month within six months, by April 2027. The monthly
goals below build to it, and the dashboard's Marketing tab tracks each
month against them (`metrics/goals.csv`). At the same pace, about 1.4 times
a month, 2,000,000 arrives around August 2027; that is a projection, not a
commitment, and Insights replaces it as the months come in.

| Month | Views | Reels posted | Views per Reel | Breakout Reels (10,000+) | Website visits from Instagram |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| November 2026 | 60,000 | 90 (3 a day) | 667 | 1 | 180 |
| December 2026 | 100,000 | 120 (4 a day) | 833 | 2 | 300 |
| January 2027 | 160,000 | 180 (6 a day) | 889 | 3 | 480 |
| February 2027 | 250,000 | 180 | 1,389 | 5 | 750 |
| March 2027 | 360,000 | 180 | 2,000 | 7 | 1,080 |
| April 2027 | 500,000 | 180 | 2,778 | 10 | 1,500 |

The steps that move views, in order of weight:

- **Volume first.** Three Reels a day in November, four in December, six from
  January. Generation is paused, so the free slots (re-cuts, phone POV,
  site recordings, licensed stock) carry November.
- **Breakouts carry the month.** By April, ten Reels a month past 10,000
  views make about half the total. Every winner gets three re-cuts the next
  week and goes into the paid plan (see How to sample).
- **Lift the typical Reel.** Views per Reel roughly quadruple over the six
  months through hook tests: one variable at a time, same footage.
- **Turn views into visits.** About 0.3% of views should reach the site; the
  bio link carries `?utm_source=instagram` so they are counted.

## The six daily slots

Two Reels a day use Higgsfield. Four cost no credits.

| Time (ET) | Slot | Made with | Credits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7:30 am | AI Reel, the day's theme | Higgsfield | about 8.5 |
| 10:00 am | Re-cut of an existing clip: new hook, new crop, new audio | Instagram or CapCut | 0 |
| 12:30 pm | Phone POV: on-screen text over real wrist and life footage | Phone | 0 |
| 3:00 pm | Explainer: the configurator on screen, a reference answered from the comments | Screen recording | 0 |
| 6:00 pm | AI Reel, the day's strongest idea, at peak time | Higgsfield | about 8.5 |
| 9:00 pm | Trend or world Reel: licensed stock footage with on-screen text | Canva stock library | 0 |

The Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday posts from `instagram-schedule.md` carry
on. The schedule's Reels are that day's AI slots, and its carousels are
extra feed posts.

**About each free slot**

- **Re-cuts.** Every generated Reel and the website clips (water, desert,
  pool, shoreline) can be cut three or four ways: a different first second,
  a different crop, different trending audio, different on-screen text. A
  re-cut of a strong Reel is the cheapest test there is.
- **Phone POV.** Real hands, real watches, real days: the desk edge, the
  car door, the gym bag. Until kits ship in November there is no film to
  show, so these sell the worry ("the first scratch") and the answer comes
  in the caption.
- **Explainers.** "Will it fit my 126610LN?" answered in 15 seconds with the
  kit finder on screen. They feed the reference-support storyline and carry
  the Rolex disclaimer.
- **Trend and world Reels.** Canva's licensed stock footage of yachts,
  hangars, cars and stables, cut to a trending sound with one line of text.
  Check each clip's licence allows commercial use, and keep the same
  no-badge, no-hull-name rule as generated media.

## How to sample

- **Log every Reel**: date, slot, format, hook (the first line on screen),
  3-second hold rate, and views at 24 hours and 72 hours.
- **Winners**: a Reel at more than twice the account's median views after 72
  hours gets three re-cuts the next week and goes into the paid plan.
- **Losers**: a format below the median for two weeks running gives its slot
  to the best-performing format.
- **Hooks**: test one variable at a time. Same footage, different first
  line, is the cleanest test.
- **Monthly check**: compare views against the six-month ramp in the
  dashboard's Marketing tab and move the following month's goal if needed.
