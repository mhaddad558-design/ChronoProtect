# Week of October 19, 2026: six a day

Storyline S6, day one. World W3, private aviation. Pattern B. The first week
of the six-a-day schedule in `../views-plan.md`: two AI Reels a day (7:30 am
and 6:00 pm) and four Reels made without credits (10:00 am, 12:30 pm,
3:00 pm, 9:00 pm). The Tuesday and Saturday carousels post at 11:00 am on
top. Times are US Eastern.

The AI Reels are made and their IDs are below. The four free Reels each day
are briefs for you to make: what to film or cut, the first line on screen,
the beats, the audio and the caption.

**Rules for the free Reels.** On-screen text in sentence case, Red Hat
Display if your editor has it, white, kept in the middle third so
Instagram's buttons do not cover it. Re-cuts use only the unbranded
ChronoProtect+ Reels listed here, never the website background clips,
which show the Rolex name on generated watches. Phone footage of your own
watch is real media: if the dial or reference shows, the caption carries
the Rolex disclaimer, and keep serial numbers and warranty-card details out
of frame. Stock footage from Canva must be licensed for commercial use, and
crop out any badge, hull name or tail number. Until kits ship in November
there is no film to show on a real watch, so phone Reels show the risk and
the caption gives the answer. Every caption ends with the preorder line
before the hashtags.

Rolex disclaimer, where needed: "Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
sponsored by Rolex SA. Reference numbers are used only to describe fitment."

Preorder line: "On preorder now, shipping in November 2026."
